# Machine-Learned Potentials for Elemental Metals and Alloys

## Overview

This benchmark is based on the study "**General-purpose machine-learned potential for 16 elemental metals and their alloys**" by Keke Song et al., published in *Nature Communications* (2024).

The study develops a unified machine-learned potential for elemental metals and their alloys. Training on elemental and binary systems is assessed against diverse compositions, physical properties and large-scale materials simulations. The central question is whether one efficient potential can retain predictive accuracy across a broad metallic chemical space.

**Reference:** Keke Song et al., *Nature Communications*, 2024. DOI: [10.1038/s41467-024-54554-x](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-54554-x).
