# NEP Infrared–Raman Spectra

## Overview

This benchmark is based on the study "**Tensorial Properties via the Neuroevolution Potential Framework: Fast Simulation of Infrared and Raman Spectra**" by Nan Xu et al., published in *Journal of Chemical Theory and Computation* (2024).

The study extends the neuroevolution-potential framework to tensorial properties such as dipole moments and polarizabilities. Learned models connect atomic configurations with infrared and Raman observables. The central question is whether efficient, symmetry-consistent tensor predictions can support accurate vibrational spectroscopy across molecular and condensed-phase systems.

**Reference:** Nan Xu et al., *Journal of Chemical Theory and Computation*, 2024. DOI: [10.1021/acs.jctc.3c01343](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jctc.3c01343).
