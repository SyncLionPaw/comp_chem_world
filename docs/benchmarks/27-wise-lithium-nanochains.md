# WiSE Lithium Nanochains

## Overview

This benchmark is based on the study "**Formation of Polymer-like Nanochains with Short Lithium–Lithium Distances in a Water-in-Salt Electrolyte**" by Kateryna Goloviznina, Alessandra Serva and Mathieu Salanne, published in *Journal of the American Chemical Society* (2024).

The study investigates polymer-like lithium nanochains in concentrated aqueous LiTFSI electrolytes. Molecular simulations and scattering measurements connect short Li–Li contacts, oxygen bridges, coordination, and collective structure. The central question is whether these observations support connected lithium aggregates and what they imply about dynamics. The tasks analyse supplied data rather than requiring new simulations.

**Reference:** Kateryna Goloviznina, Alessandra Serva and Mathieu Salanne, *Journal of the American Chemical Society*, 2024. DOI: [10.1021/jacs.3c12488](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.3c12488).
