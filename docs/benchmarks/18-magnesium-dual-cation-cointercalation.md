# Magnesium Dual Cation Cointercalation

## Overview

This benchmark is based on the study "**Improving rechargeable magnesium batteries through dual cation co-intercalation strategy**" by Ananyo Roy et al., published in *Nature Communications* (2024).

The study investigates dual-cation co-intercalation in rechargeable magnesium batteries using TiS2 hosts. Experimental characterisation and first-principles calculations examine how Li/Mg and Na/Mg combinations affect stacking, charge redistribution, diffusion, and structural stability. The central question is when a second cation can improve magnesium insertion and transport.

**Reference:** Ananyo Roy et al., *Nature Communications*, 2024. DOI: [10.1038/s41467-023-44495-2](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-44495-2).
