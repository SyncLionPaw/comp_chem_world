# Cation-Driven CO₂ Reduction

## Overview

This benchmark is based on the study "**Cation-induced changes in the inner- and outer-sphere mechanisms of electrocatalytic CO2 reduction**" by Xueping Qin et al., published in *Nature Communications* (2023).

The study investigates how alkali-metal cations alter CO2 reduction at gold–water interfaces. Constrained and slow-growth density-functional-theory molecular dynamics compare outer-sphere and inner-sphere electron-transfer pathways. The central question is how local cation coordination, hydration, and interfacial conditions affect CO2 activation and its kinetic barriers.

**Reference:** Xueping Qin et al., *Nature Communications*, 2023. DOI: [10.1038/s41467-023-43300-4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-43300-4).
