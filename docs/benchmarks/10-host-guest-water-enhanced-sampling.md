# Host–Guest Water Enhanced Sampling

## Overview

This benchmark is based on the study "**The role of water in host-guest interaction**" by Valerio Rizzi et al., published in *Nature Communications* (2021).

The study investigates how water controls host–guest interactions. Machine-learned collective variables and enhanced sampling connect solvent rearrangements, cavity hydration, and binding free energies. The central question is when changes in water organisation explain binding behaviour and how strongly finite simulation evidence supports those mechanistic conclusions.

**Reference:** Valerio Rizzi et al., *Nature Communications*, 2021. DOI: [10.1038/s41467-020-20310-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-20310-0).
