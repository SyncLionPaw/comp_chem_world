# Infrared Spectra Structure Elucidation

## Overview

This benchmark is based on the study "**Leveraging infrared spectroscopy for automated structure elucidation**" by Marvin Alberts, Teodoro Laino and Alain C. Vaucher, published in *Communications Chemistry* (2024).

The study investigates automated molecular-structure elucidation from infrared spectra using a transformer model. It connects spectral information and molecular formulas with structures, scaffolds, and functional groups. The central question is how much structural information IR spectra can support. The benchmark focuses on simulated spectra, controlled candidate sets, robustness, and evidence-based interpretation.

**Reference:** Marvin Alberts, Teodoro Laino and Alain C. Vaucher, *Communications Chemistry*, 2024. DOI: [10.1038/s42004-024-01341-w](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42004-024-01341-w).
