# Layered Materials Pressure Lattice Dynamics

## Overview

This benchmark is based on the study "**Interlayer engineering of lattice dynamics and elastic constants of 2D layered nanomaterials under pressure**" by Guoshuai Du et al., published in *Nature Communications* (2025).

The study investigates pressure-dependent interlayer vibrations and elastic properties of layered nanomaterials. Raman measurements, coupled-layer models, molecular dynamics, and density-functional calculations link shear and breathing modes to interlayer force constants. The central question is how pressure reshapes interlayer coupling, mode behaviour, and mechanical response.

**Reference:** Guoshuai Du et al., *Nature Communications*, 2025. DOI: [10.1038/s41467-025-60211-8](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-60211-8).
