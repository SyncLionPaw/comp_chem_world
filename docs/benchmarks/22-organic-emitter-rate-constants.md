# Organic Emitter Rate Constants

## Overview

This benchmark is based on the study "**Quantitative prediction of rate constants and its application to organic emitters**" by Katsuyuki Shizu and Hironori Kaji, published in *Nature Communications* (2024).

The study investigates quantitative radiative and non-radiative rate constants in organic emitters. Excited-state electronic structure, spin–orbit coupling, and population kinetics connect molecular properties with fluorescence, reverse intersystem crossing, and photoluminescence yields. The central question is how these calculations support emitter design and which rates can be inferred from time-resolved measurements.

**Reference:** Katsuyuki Shizu and Hironori Kaji, *Nature Communications*, 2024. DOI: [10.1038/s41467-024-49069-4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-49069-4).
