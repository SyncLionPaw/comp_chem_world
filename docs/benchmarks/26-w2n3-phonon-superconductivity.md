# W₂N₃ Phonon Superconductivity

## Overview

This benchmark is based on the study "**Prediction of Phonon-Mediated Superconductivity with High Critical Temperature in the Two-Dimensional Topological Semimetal W2N3**" by Davide Campi, Simran Kumari and Nicola Marzari, published in *Nano Letters* (2021).

The study predicts phonon-mediated superconductivity in a two-dimensional W2N3 monolayer using first-principles lattice dynamics and electron–phonon coupling. It examines the effects of strain and doping on the superconducting response. The central question is whether the material is dynamically stable and which calculations support a defensible superconductivity prediction.

**Reference:** Davide Campi, Simran Kumari and Nicola Marzari, *Nano Letters*, 2021. DOI: [10.1021/acs.nanolett.0c05125](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.nanolett.0c05125). Structure data: [Materials Cloud](https://doi.org/10.24435/materialscloud:2017.0008/v1), CC BY 4.0 except separately licensed pseudopotential families; the bundled PSLibrary 1.0.0 pseudopotentials are GPL-2.0-or-later.
