# Protein–Drug Quantum Refinement

## Overview

This benchmark is based on the study "**Accelerating reliable multiscale quantum refinement of protein–drug systems enabled by machine learning**" by Zeyin Yan et al., published in *Nature Communications* (2024).

The study investigates reliable multiscale quantum refinement of protein–drug structures, assisted by machine learning. Quantum-region choices, molecular geometry, electronic-state assumptions, and crystallographic evidence are used to assess refinement quality. The central question is how computational efficiency can be improved without sacrificing chemically and structurally defensible conclusions.

**Reference:** Zeyin Yan et al., *Nature Communications*, 2024. DOI: [10.1038/s41467-024-48453-4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-48453-4).
