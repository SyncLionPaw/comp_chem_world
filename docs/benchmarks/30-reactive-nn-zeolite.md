# Reactive Neural-Network Potentials for Water-Loaded Zeolites

## Overview

This benchmark is based on the study "**A reactive neural network framework for water-loaded acidic zeolites**" by Andreas Erlebach et al., published in *Nature Communications* (2024).

The study develops a reactive neural-network potential for water-loaded acidic zeolites across different water concentrations and silicon-to-aluminium ratios. Reactive sampling and extensions to higher-level electronic references address the cost of studying confined water and ions. The central question is how to describe zeolite chemistry accurately across realistic compositions and operating conditions.

**Reference:** Andreas Erlebach et al., *Nature Communications*, 2024. DOI: [10.1038/s41467-024-48609-2](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-48609-2).
