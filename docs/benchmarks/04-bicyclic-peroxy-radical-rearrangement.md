# Bicyclic Peroxy Radical Rearrangement

## Overview

This benchmark is based on the study "**Molecular rearrangement of bicyclic peroxy radicals is a key route to aerosol from aromatics**" by Siddharth Iyer et al., published in *Nature Communications* (2023).

The study investigates the rearrangement of aromatic bicyclic peroxy radicals and its role in forming oxygenated aerosol precursors. Quantum-chemical energies and master-equation kinetics connect stationary-point structures with competing ring-opening channels and atmospheric reaction conditions. The central question is when rearrangement can sustain aromatic autoxidation and aerosol formation.

**Reference:** Siddharth Iyer et al., *Nature Communications*, 2023. DOI: [10.1038/s41467-023-40675-2](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-40675-2).
