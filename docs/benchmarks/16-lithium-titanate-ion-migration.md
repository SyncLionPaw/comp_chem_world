# Lithium Titanate Ion Migration

## Overview

This benchmark is based on the study "**Kinetic pathways of ionic transport in fast-charging lithium titanate**" by Wei Zhang et al., published in *Science* (2020).

The study investigates rapid lithium transport in Li4Ti5O12 despite its two-phase lithiation reaction. Operando electron-energy-loss spectroscopy and first-principles calculations connect local lithium coordination with migration through metastable intermediates. The central question is how transient structural pathways enable fast transport that is not explained by the equilibrium end phases alone.

**Reference:** Wei Zhang et al., *Science*, 2020. DOI: [10.1126/science.aax3520](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aax3520).
