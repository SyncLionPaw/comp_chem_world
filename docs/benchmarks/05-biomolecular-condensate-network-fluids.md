# Biomolecular Condensate Network Fluids

## Overview

This benchmark is based on the study "**Biomolecular condensates form spatially inhomogeneous network fluids**" by Furqan Dar et al., published in *Nature Communications* (2024).

The study investigates condensates formed by N130 and rpL5, focusing on whether their organisation resembles a spatially inhomogeneous network fluid. Scattering, fluorescence recovery, atomistic modelling, and coarse-grained simulations connect acidic-segment interactions with molecular connectivity and mobility. The central question is how interaction heterogeneity controls condensate structure and dynamics.

**Reference:** Furqan Dar et al., *Nature Communications*, 2024. DOI: [10.1038/s41467-024-47602-z](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-47602-z).
