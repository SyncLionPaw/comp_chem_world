# Double-Layer Charging and CO2 Reduction on Gold

## Overview

This benchmark is based on the study "**Double layer charging driven carbon dioxide adsorption limits the rate of electrochemical carbon dioxide reduction on Gold**" by Stefan Ringe et al., published in *Nature Communications* (2020).

The study investigates how double-layer charging influences CO2 adsorption and electrochemical reduction rates on gold. Electrochemical measurements are combined with reaction kinetics, interfacial electrostatics and mass-transport modelling to examine potential-dependent rate limitations. The central question is how surface charging couples adsorption, reaction kinetics and transport at the electrode interface.

**Reference:** Stefan Ringe et al., *Nature Communications*, 2020. DOI: [10.1038/s41467-019-13777-z](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-019-13777-z).
