# WPD-Loop Sequence, Structure and Catalytic Activity

## Overview

This benchmark is based on the study "**Insights into the importance of WPD-loop sequence for activity and structure in protein tyrosine phosphatases**" by Ruidan Shen et al., published in *Chemical Science* (2022).

The study investigates how WPD-loop sequence affects the structure and catalytic activity of protein tyrosine phosphatases. Structural, kinetic and computational analyses connect loop variation to active-site geometry and the dynamics of other mobile loops. The central question is how sequence changes influence the relationship between conformational motion and chemical catalysis.

**Reference:** Ruidan Shen et al., *Chemical Science*, 2022. DOI: [10.1039/D2SC04135A](https://doi.org/10.1039/D2SC04135A).
