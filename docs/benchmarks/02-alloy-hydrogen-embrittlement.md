# Alloy Hydrogen Embrittlement

## Overview

This benchmark is based on the study "**Mechanism and Prediction of Hydrogen Embrittlement in fcc Stainless Steels and High Entropy Alloys**" by Xiao Zhou, Ali Tehranchi and William A. Curtin, published in *Physical Review Letters* (2021).

The study investigates hydrogen embrittlement in face-centred-cubic steels and high-entropy alloys. It combines first-principles energetics, statistical hydrogen-occupancy models, and fracture mechanics to connect local hydrogen trapping with cleavage and dislocation emission. The central question is how alloy chemistry and hydrogen distribution change intrinsic resistance to embrittlement.

**Reference:** Xiao Zhou, Ali Tehranchi and William A. Curtin, *Physical Review Letters*, 2021. DOI: [10.1103/physrevlett.127.175501](https://doi.org/10.1103/physrevlett.127.175501).
