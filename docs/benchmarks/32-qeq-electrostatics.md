# Charge Equilibration and Electrostatics in Nanoporous Materials

## Overview

This benchmark is based on the study "**Evaluating Charge Equilibration Methods To Generate Electrostatic Fields in Nanoporous Materials**" by Daniele Ongari et al., published in *Journal of Chemical Theory and Computation* (2019).

The study evaluates charge-equilibration algorithms and parameter choices for nanoporous materials against density-functional reference charges. Atom-specific charge errors are connected to electrostatic fields and gas-adsorption predictions in metal–organic frameworks. The central question is which combinations of algorithm and parameters provide reliable electrostatics for adsorption screening.

**Reference:** Daniele Ongari et al., *Journal of Chemical Theory and Computation*, 2019. DOI: [10.1021/acs.jctc.8b00669](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jctc.8b00669).
