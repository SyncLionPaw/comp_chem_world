# Hybrid DFT Extended Excited States

## Overview

This benchmark is based on the study "**Excited-State Properties for Extended Systems: Efficient Hybrid Density Functional Methods**" by Anna-Sophia Hehn et al., published in *Journal of Chemical Theory and Computation* (2022).

The study investigates efficient hybrid-density-functional approaches to excited-state properties in extended systems. Auxiliary density matrix methods and simplified response kernels are compared for molecular and periodic geometries, excitation energies, fluorescence, and cost. The central question is where computational savings preserve useful structural and optical accuracy.

**Reference:** Anna-Sophia Hehn et al., *Journal of Chemical Theory and Computation*, 2022. DOI: [10.1021/acs.jctc.2c00144](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jctc.2c00144).
