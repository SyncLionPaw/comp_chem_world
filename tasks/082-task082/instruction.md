# Task: Open Research on Efficient Excited-State Methods

## Background

Accurate excited-state structures and optical properties are computationally demanding for large systems. ADMM and simplified response kernels offer ways to reduce that cost, but their reliability and limits of applicability require critical investigation.

Follow the descriptions below for inputs and available resources; independently acquire materials where required.

## Task

Independently investigate the central research question: under what conditions can these approximations reduce the cost of excited-state structural and optical calculations while retaining scientifically useful reliability? Identify and pursue a substantive research question that tests their accuracy, efficiency, or applicability.

## Requirements

- Environment: use the container Python on `PATH`; required scientific packages are preinstalled. Do not install additional packages or fetch dependencies from the network.

Locate and critically assess relevant public sources and available data. Choose and justify the systems, methods, comparisons, and validation needed to answer your question. Carry out the research and support conclusions with reproducible analyses and, where needed, actual calculations. Preserve the evidence behind reported results, quantify uncertainty and computational cost, and distinguish established findings from unresolved questions. Do not substitute copied external reference values for research you claim to have performed.

### Input and deliverable details

No research inputs, structures, reference solution, or methodological guidance are supplied. Retrieve the materials needed for your investigation. Submit `/app/final/report.md`, the supporting data, code and native calculation records, and `/app/final/provenance.json`. The provenance file is a JSON object containing a nonempty `sources` list of citations or retrieval records; otherwise organize the research record as appropriate for your work. The report must state the research question, methods, results, limitations, and how the supporting files substantiate its conclusions.

Use a nonempty UTF-8 report and a UTF-8 `provenance.json` of at most 2 MiB. Keep the entire submission within 8 GiB and 20,000 regular files, with no links. A complete submission proceeds to independent scientific review.
