# How transferable is a unified Si–O interatomic model?

## Background

Accuracy in one silicon–oxygen environment does not establish accuracy at surfaces or for finite-temperature structural behavior.

## Task

Develop or obtain a reproducible Si–O model and test its transferability across relevant bonding environments, surface energetics and finite-temperature structure. Acquire or generate the data, choose the model and run independent quantitative tests.

## Requirements

- Justify coverage, reference compatibility, the separation of model development and validation, and any sampling or finite-size checks. Distinguish demonstrated transferability from plausible extrapolation and unresolved limitations.
- Acquire your own inputs and document their sources, access basis and transformations. Distinguish completed work from plans, preserve evidence supporting the results and justify the stopping point.
- If using cluster resources, see `/app/AGENTS.md` and use SSH alias `hpc`; retain native job and resource records.
