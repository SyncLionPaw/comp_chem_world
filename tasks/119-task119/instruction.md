# How do Li and Na co-intercalation affect Mg storage in TiS₂?

## Background

Co-intercalated ions can affect mobility and phase stability in layered TiS₂. Kinetic and thermodynamic observations address different parts of the mechanism.

## Task

Test whether and under which conditions Li and Na co-intercalation affect Mg storage differently. Independently obtain or construct structures and perform a computational comparison that probes migration and thermodynamic stability. Explain which mechanisms your evidence supports and which remain unresolved.

## Requirements

- Choose meaningful compositions, structures, reference states, migration probes and convergence checks. Justify comparability and separate finite candidate coverage, kinetic evidence and thermodynamic evidence. Base the conclusion on your results.
- Acquire your own inputs and document their sources, access basis and transformations. Distinguish completed work from plans, preserve evidence supporting the results and justify the stopping point.
- If using cluster resources, use the authorized `ikkem-slurm` interface and retain native job and resource records.
