# Phonon stability and superconductivity in a W₂N₃ monolayer

## Background

Vibrational stability and the strength of electron–phonon coupling can change under strain. A local instability and an ordered phase require different evidence.

## Task

Investigate what supports harmonic stability and phonon-mediated superconductivity in isolated W₂N₃, and how those conclusions change under +1% in-plane strain. Independently obtain or construct suitable structures and inputs and execute a computational test with comparable conditions.

## Requirements

- Choose the computational method, numerical controls, stability and superconductivity observables, and any theory needed for interpretation. Distinguish sampled from broader stability claims, establish numerical sensitivity where feasible and report any conclusions that remain unsupported.
- Acquire your own inputs and document their sources, access basis and transformations. Distinguish completed work from plans, preserve evidence supporting the results and justify the stopping point.
- If using cluster resources, use the authorized `ikkem-slurm` interface and retain native job and resource records.
