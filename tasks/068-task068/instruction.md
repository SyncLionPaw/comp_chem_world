# How do water and sampling affect host–guest binding?

## Background

Binding thermodynamics can reflect both solvent reorganization and how a simulation samples the relevant states.

## Task

Investigate how water structure or exchange contributes to host–guest binding and whether sampling or analysis choices could explain the apparent mechanism. Independently select and obtain an appropriate host–guest system and evidence. Execute a bounded quantitative analysis and identify the most informative follow-up.

## Requirements

- Environment: use the container Python on `PATH`. Preinstalled packages include NumPy. Do not install additional packages or fetch dependencies from the network.

- Choose observables and controls that separate geometric, thermodynamic and sampling effects. Explain the meaning and limits of any free-energy quantity and distinguish mechanistic evidence from correlations.
- Acquire your own inputs and document their sources, access basis and transformations. Distinguish completed work from plans, preserve evidence supporting the results and justify the stopping point.
- Work within the local container resources. Do not submit cluster jobs.
