# Case01 corrected SPC/E science plan — frozen before corrected run

SOL planned; LUNA stages and executes. Existing 20 ns trajectory is invalid as SPC/E evidence because its exact production TPR lacked intramolecular water exclusions. Its original outputs and hashes are preserved, but are not the reference curve for this corrected run. This plan applies to the staged corrected workflow only. No MD is submitted by this package-staging step.

## Native model and execution audit

The static production TPR must be generated with Bigroup GROMACS from `production.mdp`, `preflight_coordinates.gro` and `topol.template.top`, with `grompp -maxwarn 0`. Before production dynamics, inspect `gmx dump` for the actual TPR and parse its settings: MD integrator, 0.002 ps timestep, 10,000,000 steps, 300 K V-rescale thermostat with 0.5 ps coupling, no production barostat, PME with 0.9 nm Coulomb and LJ cutoffs, xyz periodicity, compressed trajectory interval 500, Cut-off/Potential-shift LJ, epsilon-r=1 and epsilon-surface=0 (conducting boundary). Independently parse all 9033 SPC/E waters in the TPR and require correct atom order/charges, SETTLE geometry, and all O-H/H-H exclusion pairs. A failed native audit stops the job before production.

Require a normally completed production log and EDR. Independently check all 20001 XTC records: 0–20000 ps at 1 ps spacing, strictly increasing times, and a fixed cubic orthogonal cell. Check all 2000 analysis frames on a 10 ps grid and assign 40 consecutive 0.5 ns statistical blocks. Verify all 27099 production atom charges and ordering. Report temperature, pressure, potential and conserved-energy series; temperature mean must be 300 ± 5 K and first/last 10 ns temperature means differ by at most 3 K. Pressure is diagnostic because NVT instantaneous pressure fluctuates substantially; it is not gated to 1 bar. Report density from the native production box and NPT-final box; both must lie within 0.90–1.10 g/cm³. This broad pre-run density gate checks a physically plausible equilibrated liquid cell. No new threshold may be chosen after results are observed.

## Independent response and statistical uncertainty

Use one frozen analysis pass over 2000 configurations and 40 contiguous 0.5 ns sufficient-statistic blocks. Compute total molecular dipoles independently, and estimate `eps_w` with the fluctuation formula. Report 10,000-draw block-bootstrap percentile 95% intervals for `eps_w`, `chi(k=0)`, low-k minimum location/height, high-k maximum location/height, and FWHM width. Record the fraction of valid bootstrap width draws; at least 95% is required for the width uncertainty gate. Conditional intervals must disclose that conditioning, and absent crossings remain unresolved. Reblock the saved sufficient statistics to 0.5, 1 and 2 ns. For `eps_w`, fixed-bin `chi` nearest 0.23, 3.02 and 5.5 Å⁻¹, and `delta_k`, adjacent SE ratios must lie in [0.65, 1.5]. First-versus-last 10 ns changes for `eps_w` and those three `chi` bins must be no larger than 3 times their combined half-run standard error. Failures remain visible and cannot be threshold-adjusted after data inspection.

The response estimator averages each reciprocal mode's deconvolved power divided by that mode's `k²` before bin averaging. Also calculate and report the legacy alternative (bin-mean power divided by bin-mean `k²`) as a definition-sensitivity diagnostic; it is not compared with the invalid old trajectory. Report the strict minimum's bootstrap support: at least 95% strict interior-local-minimum support marks it resolved. Lower support is an allowed scientific result; publish its point estimate, interval, bin/finite-size resolution and reblocking sensitivity, and label it unresolved.

## Numerical estimator checks

Use 200 deterministic configurations, ten per 1 ns block. Compare full-bin 256³/384³ estimates on the same frames, and compute exact coordinate Fourier sums for at least two nonconjugate directions per nonzero output bin plus eight directions in selected low-k, peak, half-height and tail bins. Save selected modes, seed, frame selection, per-bin paired block data and sufficient statistics.

Predetermined gates: 256-versus-384 per-bin paired 95% absolute upper discrepancy must not exceed `max(1%, 3 × full-2000-frame relative statistical SE)`. The selected direct-versus-384 paired per-bin relative bias plus its 95% paired margin must not exceed 1%. Peak position and FWHM changes between 256 and 384 must each be ≤0.05 Å⁻¹. One percent is a numerical tolerance, not a statistical confidence interval. Report grid and direct numerical effects separately and triangle-sum their simultaneous bootstrap-estimated envelopes before propagating to observables. Propagate envelopes to possible extrema and FWHM crossing bounds; an unbracketed width remains unresolved. Do not hide estimator error inside bootstrap intervals.

## Completion rule

`COMPLETE` requires actual corrected native TPR/trajectory evidence, the full native and statistical coverage above, successful preregistered numerical gates, all required outputs and provenance hashes. A scientifically unresolved low-k minimum is publishable and does not alone block coverage, provided it is explicitly marked unresolved. Any unsupported native check, missing output, failed gate or unresolved width bound keeps scientific status `PARTIAL`. Runtime binding, agent evaluation and qualification remain separate states.
